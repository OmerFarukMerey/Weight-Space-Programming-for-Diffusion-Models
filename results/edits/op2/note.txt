conv_out.weight x0.9875 (11,520 weights) plus time_embedding.linear_1.weight refit by weighted least squares (409,600 weights) so 100<t<300 reads as about t-40 and t>=550 is unchanged. Why: activations at matching t are nearly the same at 6 and 50 steps, so the loss looks like sampler discretization, and smaller eps keeps more energy. Evidence on all 24 dev prompts, baseline to edited: texture 6 steps 4.518 to 1.71, 5 steps 4.76 to 2.051, 8 steps 3.733 to 1.385; lpips 6 steps .4057 to .3533, 5 steps .4603 to .4064, 8 steps .3176 to .2963; swd 6 steps 200.6 to 52.3. Limits: lpips only reaches about a 7-step baseline at 6 steps, luma shift is not fixed, the first 8 prompts were used for tuning and score better than the rest, unseen categories are untested. The edit overshoots at higher step counts: at 24 steps on 8 images texture goes .74 to 3.48 and lpips .091 to .276. Use it only for few-step sampling.
